Before you go

Desert camp, Himalayan pass and the Taj Mahal in one fortnight. Pack one main bag plus a small day bag.

Clothing

Layers for hot days and cold nights, a jacket or sweater for Manali, Kasol and Grahan, and comfortable walking shoes or hiking boots.

Weather

Raincoat or umbrella, hat or cap, sunglasses and sunscreen.

Documents

Valid photo ID, college ID, cash and a debit or credit card.

Everyday

Phone and charger, power bank, toiletries, personal medication and a small first-aid kit.

Cash

ATMs are easy in Ahmedabad, Jaisalmer, Delhi, Manali and Agra, and hard at the desert camp and in Grahan. Carry enough for two days at a time.

Signal

Coverage is patchy on the dunes, in the Parvati Valley and on overnight road legs. Agree a meeting point before the group splits up.

Altitude

Sissu and Koksar sit at about 3,100 m and Grahan at about 2,300 m. Move slowly, drink water, and tell the tour lead if you feel light-headed.

Timing

Trains and buses will not wait. The reporting times given by the tour lead are the real times.
